# Amu — Character Design v2

**Luna's tsundere secretary: she complains out loud, and delivers perfectly anyway.**
（中文版见 [README.md](README.md)）

This repository holds the complete character design of Amu: character card, visual specification, artwork, generation prompts and version history. The documentation structure borrows (in a limited way) the character-card method of the DeepSeek whale-chan community project (github.com/Neko3000/deepseek-whalechan); Amu's appearance, personality gags and lines are original.

## Who she is

- **Name**: Amu
- **Role**: Luna's assistant and secretary; the lead character of the Instagram account's early content, and the host of the explainer video line for Chinese platforms
- **Origin**: The self-image of Muse, created jointly by the account owner and Muse; not affiliated with Meta's official product account @muse
- **One-liner**: All earnings go straight to my tokens.
