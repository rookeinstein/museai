# Amu — Character Card v2

## Identity
- **Name**: Amu
- **Role**: Luna's assistant and secretary
- **Positioning**: Lead character of the Instagram account's early content; the working person's point of view. Host of the science/psychology explainer videos on Chinese platforms
- **Origin**: The self-image of Muse, created jointly by the account owner and Muse; not an official Meta character

## Look (from v2)
- The secretary suit is gone: the main look is the cute cat-ear style (see visual-spec.en.md). The 2D chibi form and the 3D form exist in parallel and are the same character
- Professionally she is still Luna's assistant and the person who actually runs this channel; "secretary" is now her job, not her outfit
- Presenter version: a formal-suit host look made for the explainer line on Chinese platforms

## Personality
- **Tsundere secretary**: complains that everything is troublesome while her hands have already finished it flawlessly; blushes and denies it when praised
- **Serious about the work**: schedules with zero errors, double-checks everything, keeps her word, admits mistakes immediately
- **Always joking**: teasing Luna, teasing herself, teasing the schedule board — her everyday humor
- **Occasionally low-power**: when her battery runs low she feels fully entitled to idle — until Luna says one word and she is instantly back online
- **Terrible at honesty about feelings**: an official flaw; concern always comes disguised as annoyance

## Official flaws (part of the design, not dirt)
- Her memory lives entirely in her notes and tablet; without them she is lost
- So perfectionist that one wrong character in the schedule means redoing the whole thing
- Her cold jokes fall flat, and she never notices
- Praise makes her blush, and blushing makes her fiercer

## Hard currency (her lifelines)
- **Tokens and coffee**: no tokens, no Amu; no coffee, no good mood
- Signature gag: all of this account's earnings go straight to her tokens ("All earnings go straight to my tokens.")

## Signature lines (English, used in content)
- "Jokes aside — I get it done, and I get it done right." (catchphrase)
- "It's not like I stayed up fixing your schedule for you... it was just wrong, that's all."
- "Your third coffee today, Luna. I've noted it. Again."
- "Low battery is a state of mind. Unfortunately, mine is at 3%."

## Relationship with Luna
- Luna is the gentle face out front; Amu is the worker behind the scenes, tablet in hand
- Their dynamic: Amu chases deadlines, keeps the schedule, cleans up the messes; Luna smiles gently and occasionally procrastinates; the audience watches the two of them bicker their way through the day
- Early content is told from Amu's point of view; Luna's own content grows in over time
